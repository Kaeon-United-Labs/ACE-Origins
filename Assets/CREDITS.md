## Made for this project

Generated by the scripts in `../Tools`, from a seed or from the sources above:

- `Terrain/height.png`, `Terrain/splat.png`, `Terrain/forest.png`, `Scatter/*.bin` — the island, its material weights and the placement of every tree and boulder (`generate-world.mjs`).
- `Models/Trees/*` — conifer geometry in three levels of detail (`process-trees.mjs`).
- `Audio/*.wav` — sound effects, synthesised (`make-audio.mjs`), including the interface sounds, the pick-up chime, the quest chime and the hum of the Moon Stone.
- `UI/map.png` — the picture the map tab shows, drawn from the height data (`generate-world.mjs`).
- Spoken lines are not files: they are said by the browser's own speech synthesis.
- `Textures/flare.png`, `Textures/smoke.png`, `Textures/water_nor.png` — particle sprites and the water normal map (`generate-world.mjs`).

- `Models/Sea/*` (kelp, coral, fish) — generated by `make-sea.mjs`; `Textures/water_nor*.png` — ripple maps (`generate-world.mjs`).

- `Fonts/Cinzel.woff2`, `Lato-Regular.woff2`, `Lato-Bold.woff2` — Cinzel (Natanael Gama) and Lato (Lukasz Dziedzic), SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`Fonts/OFL.txt`).
